## What is the Artwork Component?

The Artwork Component lets you offer customers a **live, interactive framing experience** on your product page. Customers can select size, frame style, and mat options — and see the artwork update in real time before they buy.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Who is it for?" icon="user">
    Artists and photographers selling **pre-made artwork**. Your images are fixed — customers choose how to frame them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What customers see" icon="eye">
    A live product preview that updates instantly as they pick sizes, frames, and mat colours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What you control" icon="settings">
    Sizes, frame styles, mat options, pricing rules, and which combinations are available.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  The Artwork Component uses your existing artwork images. Customers cannot upload their own photos here. For customer uploads, see the [Upload Component](/livevue/upload-component).
</Info>

## Accessing the Artwork Component

![LIVEARf dashboard — navigate to Components in the left sidebar](/images/artwork/dashboard.png)

1. Open the **LIVEARf app** from your Shopify admin
2. In the left sidebar, click **Components**
3. Select an existing **Artwork Component** or click **Create My Own**
4. Open the **Basic Configuration** tab

You will see two panels side by side:
- **Left panel** — all your configuration settings
- **Right panel** — a live **Preview Product** that updates as you make changes

<Tip>
  Always keep an eye on the Preview Product panel on the right as you configure. It reflects every change you make in real time so you can catch mistakes before saving.
</Tip>

---

## Component Header Settings

At the very top of the configuration screen you will find three key settings that control the component's basic behaviour.

![Component header — showing Component Name, Image Upload toggle, Quick Setup Guide, and Component Status](/images/artwork/component-header.png)

| Setting | What it does | Visible to customers? |
|---|---|---|
| **Component Name** | Internal label to identify this component in your admin | No |
| **Image Upload** | Enables or disables customer image upload. Keep **OFF** for Artwork Components — your artwork is pre-defined | No |
| **Component Status** | Toggle **ON** to activate. Toggle **OFF** to pause without deleting | No |

<Warning>
  Always click **Save** in the top-right corner after making any changes. Navigating away without saving will lose all unsaved work.
</Warning>

## Options Panel

The Options panel defines every customisation choice your customers will see on the product page.

![Options panel — showing Shopify Options list on the left and live Preview Product on the right](/images/artwork/options-list.png)

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Shopify Options">
    The first three options in the list are **automatically inherited from your Shopify product variants**. They represent your existing product options such as Size.

    - Use the **eye icon** next to each option to show or hide it on the storefront
    - Hidden options do not appear in the Preview Product panel
    - These cannot be deleted — they are pulled directly from your Shopify product

    <Note>
      If your Shopify product has no variants set up, these three slots will appear empty. Set up your product variants in Shopify before configuring here.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Custom Options (LIVEARf)">
    LIVEARf automatically creates three custom options for you:

    | Option | Purpose |
    |---|---|
    | **Frames** | Frame style selection shown as visual swatches |
    | **Mat Size** | Controls the spacing between the artwork and the frame |
    | **Mat Colour** | Controls the colour of the mat border |

    Each custom option can be **edited**, **duplicated**, or **deleted** at any time using the icons on the right of each row.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Configuring an Option

Every option — whether Shopify-inherited or custom — follows the same configuration flow. Click any option name in the list on the left to open its settings.

### Step 1 — Option Name and Label

![Option Name (red box, internal) vs Option Label (green box, customer-facing)](/images/artwork/option-name-label.png)

| Field | What it does | Shown to customers? |
|---|---|---|
| **Option Name** | Internal reference only — used in rules and configuration | No |
| **Option Label** | The text customers see above the option on the product page | Yes |
| **Hide toggle** | Hides the option entirely from the storefront | — |

---

### Step 2 — Choose an Option Type

The Option Type controls how customers interact with this option.

![Option Type dropdown open — showing all 6 available types: Swatch, Rectangle, Radio Button, Number, Colour, Dropdown](/images/artwork/option-type-dropdown.png)

| Type | Best used for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Swatch** | Frame images or mat colour thumbnails | Gold, White, Wood, Black frames |
| **Rectangle** | Size options shown as pill buttons | 10×10, 20×20, 30×30 |
| **Radio Button** | Single-choice text selections | Orientation: Portrait, Landscape |
| **Dropdown** | Long lists of choices | 10+ size options |
| **Number** | Custom numeric input | Custom width or height in cm |
| **Colour** | Colour picker | Mat colour |

<Note>
  The Preview Product updates instantly when you change the Option Type — use this to see exactly how it will look to customers before saving.
</Note>

---

### Step 3 — Assign a Section

The Section tells LIVEVUE what this option represents. It controls how the option renders visually and which Actions become available.

![Section dropdown open — showing all available sections: Frame, Mat, Canvas, Acrylic, Metal, Wood, Additional, Pricing, Units, None](/images/artwork/section-dropdown.png)

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Frame** | Frame border visualisation and styles |
| **Mat** | Mat appearance around the artwork (visual only) |
| **Canvas** | Canvas wrap rendering — how edges are handled |
| **Acrylic** | Acrylic mounting style options |
| **Metal** | Metal print style options |
| **Wood** | Wood-based finish options |
| **Custom Size** | Enables custom width and height input fields |
| **Custom Mat Size** | Enables custom mat spacing input |
| **Pricing** | Adds a pricing adjustment to this option |
| **Additional** | Optional add-ons (e.g. hanging kit, certificate) |
| **None** | No special visual behaviour |

**Example — Frame section selected:**

![Section set to Frame — frame border wraps around artwork in the Preview Product](/images/artwork/section-frame.png)

**Example — Mat section selected:**

![Section set to Mat — mat spacing applied around artwork in the Preview Product](/images/artwork/section-mat.png)

---

### Step 4 — Configure Actions

Some sections unlock an **Action** dropdown for each option value. Actions define how that specific value renders visually in the preview.

![Action dropdown open — showing Standard Frame, Infinity Frame, Floater Frame options for the Frame section](/images/artwork/action-dropdown.png)

| Section | Available Actions |
|---|---|
| **Frame** | Standard Frame, Infinity Frame, Floater Frame |
| **Canvas** | Classic Wrap, Mirror, Colour, Blur |
| **Acrylic** | Spacers, Mount, Sandwich |
| **Metal** | Print, Float Mount |
| **Wood** | Wooden Pallet, Box Board |
| **Pricing** | Discount, Margin, Markup |

<Note>
  Sections like **Mat** do not support Actions — the Action dropdown will be greyed out automatically. This is expected behaviour.
</Note>

---

### Step 5 — Add Option Values

Option values are the individual choices available to customers — for example Gold, White, Wood, and Black for the Frames option.

For each value you can:
- Set a **Label** (the text customers see)
- Upload a **Main image** (full frame used in the visualisation) and a **Thumbnail** (small swatch shown in the selector)
- Mark one value as the **Default** selection
- Set a **Price** for this specific value
- **Duplicate** or **Delete** the value

All values appear visually in the Preview Product panel on the right as you add them.

## Mat, Pricing & Advanced Configuration

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="Mat Size & Colour">

    ### Mat Size

    The Mat Size option controls the spacing between the artwork edge and the inner frame edge. LIVEVUE supports both **single mat** and **double mat** configurations.

    ![Mat Size option — showing option name set to Mat Size, Action dropdown, and 3 values: 2 inch, 1 inch, No Mat](/images/artwork/mat-size-config.png)

    Each mat layer has its own three sub-settings, available in the Action dropdown:

    ![Mat Size action dropdown — showing First Mat Size, First Mat Style, First Mat Colour, Second Mat Size, Second Mat Style, Second Mat Colour](/images/artwork/mat-size-actions.png)

    | Sub-setting | Purpose |
    |---|---|
    | **First Mat Size** | Width of the primary mat border |
    | **First Mat Style** | Visual style of the primary mat |
    | **First Mat Colour** | Colour of the primary mat |
    | **Second Mat Size** | Width of the secondary (inner) mat border |
    | **Second Mat Style** | Visual style of the secondary mat |
    | **Second Mat Colour** | Colour of the secondary mat |

    <Tip>
      To use a single mat only, configure the First Mat settings and set the Second Mat values to zero — or hide them entirely using a Conditional Rule in the Advanced Configuration tab.
    </Tip>

    Mat Size supports all pricing methods. Click the **edit icon** on the right of any value to configure its price.

    ---

    ### Mat Colour

    The Mat Colour option controls the visual colour of the mat shown in the live preview.

    ![Mat Colour option — showing Swatch type selected, Mat section, and Black/White colour values configured](/images/artwork/mat-colour-config.png)

    - Uses the same six mat action sub-settings as Mat Size
    - Supports all pricing methods
    - Use the **image icon** on each value to upload or select a colour swatch
    - Changes reflect immediately in the Preview Product panel on the right

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Pricing">

    ### Pricing Methods

    Every option value can have a price attached to it. LIVEVUE supports eight pricing methods across two categories — choose the one that matches how you charge for each option.

    ![Pricing configuration — Price Type dropdown highlighted with option values and prices set](/images/artwork/pricing-config.png)

    ![Standard Pricing dropdown open — showing Fixed Price, Surface Area, Perimeter, Linear Unit options](/images/artwork/standard-pricing.png)

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Standard Pricing">
        Standard pricing **calculates automatically** based on the actual artwork dimensions at the time of purchase.

        | Method | How it calculates | Best for |
        |---|---|---|
        | **Fixed Price (Standard)** | A flat amount added per selection, regardless of size | Frame styles, mat colours |
        | **Surface Area (Standard)** | Price × artwork width × artwork height | Canvas, metal, acrylic prints |
        | **Perimeter (Standard)** | Price × total edge length of the artwork | Frame moulding priced per linear inch |
        | **Linear Unit (Standard)** | Price per unit of one dimension | Mat border width |

        **Example:** A Gold frame set to Surface Area at $0.05/sq inch will automatically charge more for a 40×40 than a 10×10.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Predefined Pricing">
        Predefined pricing lets you **manually set a specific price for each size variant** — useful when your prices do not follow a simple formula.

        | Method | How it calculates | Best for |
        |---|---|---|
        | **Fixed Price (Predefined)** | A different flat price per size variant | Hand-crafted frames with irregular pricing |
        | **Surface Area (Predefined)** | Surface area calculation per size | Custom price points per size |
        | **Perimeter (Predefined)** | Perimeter calculation per size | Size-dependent frame pricing |
        | **Linear Unit (Predefined)** | Linear unit per size | Size-dependent mat pricing |

        **Example:** A 10×10 frame costs $25, a 20×20 costs $60, a 40×40 costs $150 — not a simple formula, so Predefined lets you set each price manually.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    ---

    ### Tooltip and Description

    All pricing methods support two optional customer-facing fields:

    | Field | Where it appears | Purpose |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Tooltip** | On hover over the option label | Short explanation (e.g. "Price varies by size") |
    | **Description** | Below the option on the product page | Longer explanation of what's included |

    {/* [ gap — screenshot of Tooltip and Description fields needed ] */}

    Use the **(i) icon** next to Frame Profile Width and Frame Rabbet Depth fields for a visual measurement guide showing customers exactly what is being measured.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Advanced Config — Rules">

    ### What are Conditional Rules?

    Conditional Rules are the most powerful feature in the Artwork Component. They automatically **show, hide, or disable options** based on what a customer has already selected — keeping your product page clean and preventing invalid or confusing combinations.

    ![Conditional Rules panel in the Advanced Configuration tab — showing WHEN / IS / THEN rule builder](/images/artwork/conditional-rules.png)

    ---

    ### How to access

    1. Click the **Advanced Configuration** tab at the top of the component screen
    2. Expand the **Conditional Rules** section
    3. Click **Add New Rule**

    ---

    ### How rules work

    Every rule follows a simple three-part structure:

    ```
    WHEN  [Option]  [Condition]  [Value]
    THEN  [Action]  [Target Option]  [Target Values]
    ```

    | Part | What you set | Available options |
    |---|---|---|
    | **WHEN — Option** | Which option triggers the rule | Frames, Mat Size, Mat Colour, any custom option |
    | **WHEN — Condition** | How to evaluate it | Equals to, Not equals to, Greater than, Less than |
    | **WHEN — Value** | What value triggers it | Any, or specific values (e.g. Black, Gold, 2 inch) |
    | **THEN — Action** | What happens when the condition is met | Show, Hide, StrikeOut |
    | **THEN — Target** | Which option is affected | Frames, Mat Colour, any custom option |
    | **THEN — Target Values** | Which values are affected | All values, or specific ones |

    ---

    ### The 3 actions explained

    {/* [ gap — screenshot showing Show / Hide / StrikeOut in action on preview product ] */}

    | Action | What it does | When to use it |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Show** | Makes a previously hidden option or value visible | Reveal options only when they become relevant |
    | **Hide** | Completely removes an option or value from the page | Remove combinations that don't apply |
    | **StrikeOut** | Keeps the value visible but crossed out and unselectable | Show that an option exists but is unavailable with the current selection |

    <Info>
      **Show vs Hide:** If you want an option hidden by default and only visible under certain conditions, start by hiding it in the Basic Configuration (use the Hide toggle on the option), then use a Show rule to reveal it when needed.
    </Info>

    ---

    ### Real examples

    <AccordionGroup>

      <Accordion title="Example 1 — Hide Mat Colour when Black frame is selected">
        **The scenario:** Your Black frame looks best without any mat. Showing Mat Colour options when Black is selected causes confusion and unwanted orders.

        **The rule:**
        ```
        WHEN  Frames  equals  Black
        THEN  Hide  Mat Colour  (all values)
        ```

        **What happens:** As soon as a customer clicks the Black frame swatch, the Mat Colour selector disappears from the page completely. If they switch to another frame, it reappears automatically.

        {/* [ gap — screenshot showing Mat Colour hidden when Black frame is selected ] */}
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Example 2 — StrikeOut unavailable frames for large sizes">
        **The scenario:** Your Floater Frame is only available up to 20×20. For larger sizes you want customers to see it exists — but make it clear they cannot select it for that size.

        **The rule:**
        ```
        WHEN  Size  greater than  20×20
        THEN  StrikeOut  Frames  (Floater Frame only)
        ```

        **What happens:** On sizes 30×30 and above, the Floater Frame swatch appears with a line through it. Customers understand it is unavailable for that size without needing to contact you.

        {/* [ gap — screenshot showing Floater Frame struck out on a large size selection ] */}
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Example 3 — Show only matching frames when 2 inch mat is selected">
        **The scenario:** Your Gold and White frames are the only ones that look right with a 2 inch mat. You want to show only those two frames when that mat size is chosen.

        **The rule:**
        ```
        WHEN  Mat Size  equals  2 inch
        THEN  Show  Frames  (Gold, White only)
        ```

        **What happens:** When a customer selects the 2 inch mat, only Gold and White frame swatches are visible. All other frames are hidden, making the decision simple and avoiding mismatched orders.

        {/* [ gap — screenshot showing only Gold and White frames visible with 2 inch mat selected ] */}
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Example 4 — Hide Second Mat options when No Mat is selected">
        **The scenario:** You offer a double mat option. When a customer selects No Mat, the Second Mat Size and Mat Colour options are completely irrelevant and clutter the page.

        **Rule 1:**
        ```
        WHEN  Mat Size  equals  No Mat
        THEN  Hide  Mat Colour  (all values)
        ```

        **Rule 2:**
        ```
        WHEN  Mat Size  equals  No Mat
        THEN  Hide  Second Mat Size  (all values)
        ```

        **What happens:** Selecting No Mat instantly removes all mat-related options from the page, giving customers a much cleaner experience when they do not want a mat.

        {/* [ gap — screenshot showing clean product page with No Mat selected — mat options hidden ] */}
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Example 5 — Show canvas wrap options only for Canvas products">
        **The scenario:** You sell both framed prints and canvas prints. Wrap Style options (Classic Wrap, Mirror Wrap) only apply to canvas. Showing them on framed products creates confusion.

        **The rule:**
        ```
        WHEN  Frames  equals  Canvas
        THEN  Show  Wrap Style  (all values)
        ```

        **What happens:** The Wrap Style option only appears when the customer selects Canvas as their frame option — keeping framed product pages completely clean of irrelevant options.

        {/* [ gap — screenshot showing Wrap Style appearing only when Canvas is selected ] */}
      </Accordion>

    </AccordionGroup>

    ---

    ### Managing and testing rules

    <Info>
      Rules run **in order from top to bottom**. If two rules conflict, the one listed lower wins. Drag rules up or down to reorder them.
    </Info>

    | Action | How to do it |
    |---|---|
    | **Reorder rules** | Drag the rule row up or down |
    | **Duplicate a rule** | Click the duplicate icon — saves time when building similar rules |
    | **Delete a rule** | Click the delete icon on the rule row |
    | **Add multiple conditions** | Use the Operator field (AND / OR) to add a second condition to one rule |
    | **Apply rules** | Click **Sync** after every change |
    | **Test rules** | Use the Preview Product panel — select different options and verify rules trigger correctly |

    <Warning>
      Always test every rule in the Preview Product panel before saving. Rules only go live on your store once the component is saved and linked to a product.
    </Warning>

  </Tab>

</Tabs>

## Preview & Saving

Before saving, use the **Preview Product** panel on the right to verify everything is correct.

![Preview Product panel highlighted — showing live size, frame, and mat selection updating in real time](/images/artwork/preview-validation.png)

Work through this checklist before clicking Save:

- All option types display correctly — swatches, rectangles, dropdowns render as expected
- Prices are calculating correctly for each option combination
- Conditional rules are triggering — options show, hide, and strikeout as configured
- The live artwork preview updates properly with each selection change
- Default values are set correctly for each option

When everything looks right, click **Save** in the top-right corner.

<Check>
  Saved successfully? Your component is ready to link to products.
</Check>

## Linking the Component to Products

A saved component is not visible to customers until it is linked to one or more products.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the component">
    Make sure **Component Status** is turned **ON** and all changes are saved. The **Link** button only becomes available once the component is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Link panel">
    Click **Link** in the top-right corner of the component configuration screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to apply it">
    | Option | What it does |
    |---|---|
    | **Selected Collections** | Applies to all products in the chosen Shopify collections |
    | **Selected Products** | Applies only to individually selected products |
    | **All Products (LIVEVUE Template Enabled)** | Applies to every product already using a LIVEVUE template |

    Click **Link** to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the link">
    Go to **LIVEARf → Components**. Your component will be listed with the number of linked products and a status of **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
