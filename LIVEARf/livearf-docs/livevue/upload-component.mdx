## What is the Upload Component?

The Upload Component is designed for **custom framers and print-on-demand businesses** that want to let customers bring their own photos or images and see exactly how they will look framed — before placing an order.

Unlike the Artwork Component, where the imagery is pre-defined by the merchant, the Upload Component puts the image in the customer's hands.

<Info>
  The Upload Component follows the same configuration structure as the Artwork Component. If you have already set up an Artwork Component, this will feel familiar. The key difference is a single toggle explained below.
</Info>

---

## When to Use the Upload Component

| Use case | Component to use |
|---|---|
| Selling your own artwork or photography | [Artwork Component](/livevue/artwork-component) |
| Customers frame their own photos | **Upload Component** |
| Print-on-demand with custom images | **Upload Component** |
| Mixed store (your art + customer uploads) | Both components on separate products |

---

## Accessing the Upload Component

1. Open the **LIVEARf app** from your Shopify admin
2. Navigate to **Components** in the left-hand menu
3. Click **Create My Own** and select **Upload Component**, or choose an existing one
4. Open the **Basic Configuration** tab

![Templates screen showing Upload Component and Artwork Component side by side with Preview and Use buttons](/images/upload/upload-component-select.jpg)

<Tip>
  Click **Preview** on the Upload Component template to see what the customer experience will look like before committing to it.
</Tip>

![Templates screen with the Upload Component Preview button highlighted](/images/upload/upload-templates.jpg)

## The Key Difference: Image Upload Toggle

The only structural difference between the Upload Component and the Artwork Component is the **Image Upload toggle**.

<ParamField path="Image Upload" type="toggle">
  **Enabled by default** in the Upload Component. This is what allows customers to upload their own image directly on the product page. Do not disable this toggle unless you intentionally want to show only a placeholder image.
</ParamField>

![Upload Component header showing Component Name, Image Upload toggle turned ON, Quick Setup Guide, and Component Status — all highlighted](/images/upload/upload-component-header.jpg)

Everything else — options, sections, pricing, conditional rules, and linking — works identically to the Artwork Component.

---

## How Customers Experience the Upload Component

When a customer lands on a product page using the Upload Component, they follow one of two paths:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Path 1 — Upload Image (Primary)">
    This is the main intended flow:

    1. Customer arrives on the product page
    2. They click the upload area and select a photo from their device
    3. Their image appears immediately in the live preview
    4. They adjust size, frame style, and mat options
    5. The preview updates in real time with their photo inside the chosen frame
    6. They add to cart and place their order

    ![Customer-facing Upload Product page showing the Upload Your Image button and live framing preview](/images/upload/upload-preview-window.png)

    This gives customers full confidence in what they are ordering before they buy.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Path 2 — Default Placeholder (Optional)">
    If a customer wants to explore framing options before uploading:

    1. A placeholder image is shown in the preview on page load
    2. Customer can browse and select frame and mat options
    3. Before adding to cart, they are prompted to upload their image
    4. The image replaces the placeholder in the preview

    This path is useful for customers who want to explore your frame offerings first.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Configuration Steps

Because the Upload Component shares its full configuration with the Artwork Component, all the following apply identically. Refer to the linked sections for detailed instructions.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Component Header Settings">
    Set the **Component Name**, confirm **Image Upload is ON**, and turn **Component Status ON**.

    See [Component Header Settings](/livevue/artwork-component#component-header-settings) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Options Panel — Shopify & Custom Options">
    Configure inherited Shopify options and custom options (Frames, Mat Size, Mat Colour) the same way as the Artwork Component.

    See [Options Panel](/livevue/artwork-component#options-panel) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sections & Actions">
    Assign sections (Frame, Mat, Canvas, Acrylic, Metal, Wood, Pricing, etc.) and configure available actions per section.

    See [Configuring an Option](/livevue/artwork-component#configuring-an-option) for the full steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pricing Configuration">
    All four pricing methods (Fixed Price, Predefined Price, Surface Area, Perimeter) are available exactly as in the Artwork Component.

    See [Pricing Configuration](/livevue/artwork-component#pricing-configuration) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Advanced Configuration — Conditional Rules">
    Conditional rules (IF → THEN logic) work identically to the Artwork Component.

    See [Advanced Configuration](/livevue/artwork-component#advanced-configuration--conditional-rules) for full details and examples.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

### Preview & Saving

Use the **Preview Product** panel on the right to verify that the Upload image prompt appears correctly and that all frame and mat options update as expected.

![Preview Product panel highlighted on the right side of the component editor](/images/upload/upload-product-preview.png)

When everything looks correct, click **Save** in the top-right corner.

## Linking the Upload Component to Products

Once your component is configured and saved, link it to the products where you want the upload experience to appear.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the component">
    Confirm **Component Status** is **ON** and all changes are saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Link panel">
    Click **Link** in the top-right corner of the configuration screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your linking method">
    | Option | What it does |
    |---|---|
    | **Selected Collections** | Applies to all products in chosen Shopify collections |
    | **Selected Products** | Applies to individually chosen products only |
    | **All Products (LIVEVUE Template Enabled)** | Applies to every product using a LIVEVUE template |

    Click **Link** to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify in Components list">
    Go to **LIVEARf → Components**. Confirm the component shows the correct number of linked products and a status of **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Upload Component Merchants

<Tip>
  Set a high-quality placeholder image that shows off your best frame. Customers who explore before uploading are more likely to convert when they can already visualise the product.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Use conditional rules to hide certain frame styles that don't suit certain sizes. This reduces customer confusion and returns.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Ensure customers are prompted to upload before checkout. If an order comes through without an image attached, follow up with the customer immediately via the order notes.
</Warning>
