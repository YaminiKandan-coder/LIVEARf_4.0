## Overview

Setting up LIVEVUE takes about 10–15 minutes. This guide walks you through the full 8-step onboarding flow — from installing the app to linking your first component to a product.

<Info>
  LIVEVUE runs entirely inside your Shopify admin. You do not need a separate account or login.
</Info>

---

## Before You Begin

Make sure you have the following ready:

- An active Shopify store
- Admin access to your Shopify account
- At least one product published in your store (for testing the preview)

---

## Installation Steps

<Steps>

  <Step title="Welcome Screen">
    Once you install the LIVEARf app from the Shopify App Store, open it from your Shopify admin panel.

    ![LIVEARf app dashboard showing the Components menu, Create Components button, and Theme Block Status: Active](/images/setup/setup-dashboard.png)

    You will see the LIVEVUE welcome screen. Click **Next** to begin the initial configuration.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Accept Terms & Generate Sample Data">
    You will be shown the Terms and Conditions screen. This step creates temporary sample data in your store so you can preview LIVEVUE without touching your real products.

    The following sample items will be created:
    - Sample images on file
    - Sample Preview Product (unlisted — not visible to customers)
    - Sample Collections

    Check the **I Agree** checkbox and click **Create**.

    Once the process completes successfully, click **Next**.

    <Warning>
      The sample product is unlisted and will not appear in your live store. It is used only for testing purposes during setup.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Your Business Role">
    Choose the role that best represents your business. This determines which default component and options are pre-loaded for you.

    | Role | Best for |
    |---|---|
    | **Artist / Seller** | Selling your own paintings, prints, or original artwork |
    | **Framer / Seller** | Custom picture framing services |
    | **Photographer / Seller** | Selling digital photography or prints |

    Select your role and click **Save**, then click **Next**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the Visualization Reference Video">
    A reference video is provided to show you what the customer-facing experience looks like — including how frame, mat, and size options update the preview in real time.

    Watch the video to understand what you are building toward, then click **Next**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a Sample Component">
    A component is the configuration block that controls how your products are visualized and customized. Select the component type based on your role:

    | Component | Select if you are... |
    |---|---|
    | **Artwork Component** | An Artist or Photographer selling pre-made work |
    | **Upload Component** | A Framer or print-on-demand business |

    ![Templates screen showing Upload Component and Artwork Component templates with Preview and Use buttons](/images/setup/setup-templates.jpg)

    A popup window will appear with automatically generated sample frame and mat options. Click **Save** to confirm the component.

    <Tip>
      You can use this sample component for testing and replace it with your own custom component at any time after setup.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create & Enable the LIVEVUE Template">
    A template controls how LIVEVUE appears on your Shopify product pages.

    1. Click **Create** — the LIVEVUE template is generated automatically.
    2. Click **Enable** to activate it.
    3. A new window will open inside Shopify. Click **Save** in the top-right corner.

    You will now see the template listed with an **Enabled** status.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link the Template to a Product">
    Use the **Switch Template** feature to assign your LIVEVUE template to the product you want to preview.

    Once linked, click **Preview Product** to verify the visualization is working correctly. You should see your sample component — sizes, frames, and mat options — updating the product preview in real time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Setup Complete">
    LIVEVUE is now enabled on your store.

    From here you can:
    - Create your own custom components tailored to your products
    - Link components to individual products, collections, or all LIVEVUE-enabled products
    - Configure advanced pricing rules and conditional logic

    <Check>
      Your store is ready. Head to the [Artwork Component](/livevue/artwork-component) or [Upload Component](/livevue/upload-component) guide to start building your real configuration.
    </Check>
  </Step>

</Steps>
